# 🚚 Logistics Delay Prediction & Strategic Optimization

An end-to-end data science project simulating real-world supply chain operations, predicting shipment delays using machine learning, and defining actionable business KPIs.

---

## 📌 Project Overview
Supply chain bottlenecks and unexpected delivery delays cost businesses millions annually. This project demonstrates how data-driven workflows can predict shipment risks before they happen and optimize routing decisions.

### Key Highlights
- **Synthetic Data Engine**: Simulates realistic multi-modal shipment data factoring in distance, traffic density, weather disruptions, and transit times.
- **Predictive Modeling**: Trains a Random Forest Classifier to identify high-risk routes and forecast delivery delays.
- **Business Intelligence**: Outlines strategic enterprise roadmaps, root-cause analyses, and critical logistics KPIs (OTIF, SLA adherence).

---

## 🎯 What This Solves
1. **Early Risk Detection**: Flag shipments likely to miss their delivery window.
2. **Operational Visibility**: Isolate whether delays stem from weather, congestion, or carrier inefficiencies.
3. **Actionable Planning**: Connect raw operational data to high-level strategic decisions.
